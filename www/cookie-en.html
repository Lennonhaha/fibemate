Cookie Policy

Version v3.0.0-DRAFT | Effective: May 16, 2026

1. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help the site remember your preferences, improve user experience, and ensure security. FIBEMATE follows the principles of minimization and transparency in cookie usage.

2. Types of Cookies We Use

2.1 Essential Cookies

3. FIBEMATE's Special Commitment

As an end-to-end encrypted communication app, FIBEMATE specifically commits to:

  • No tracking cookies: We do not track your browsing behavior across sites
  • No advertising cookies: We display no ads, so no ad tracking is needed
  • No third-party sharing: Cookie data is used only for service functionality
  • Encrypted storage: Sensitive cookie values are encrypted
  • Local-first: Most preferences are stored locally and not uploaded to servers

4. Managing Cookies

You can manage cookies through:

  • In-app settings: FIBEMATE Settings > Privacy > Cookie Management
  • Browser settings: Manage cookies in your browser preferences
  • First visit: We display a cookie consent banner on your first visit

Please note: Disabling essential cookies may prevent the service from functioning properly.

5. Local Storage Technologies

In addition to cookies, FIBEMATE uses the following local storage technologies:

  • IndexedDB: Stores encryption key material and local message cache
  • LocalStorage: Stores UI preferences and session state
  • Session Storage: Stores temporary session data

All local storage data is protected by end-to-end encryption; servers cannot access plaintext content.

6. Policy Updates

We may update this Cookie Policy periodically. Significant changes will be communicated through in-app notifications or email. Continued use of the service constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
