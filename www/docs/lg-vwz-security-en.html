LookingGlass / VWZ — Security Assessment

FIBEMATE Technical Documentation · 2026-06-30 · Public Transparency

LookingGlass v2

LookingGlass v2 is an algebraic isolation lattice-hardening architecture built on finite group representation theory. It constructs a seven-layer direct-sum system of pairwise orthogonal, inequivalent irreducible representations. By Schur's lemma, no non-trivial inter-layer linear mapping exists, fundamentally eliminating sublattice decomposition and divide-and-conquer BKZ shortcuts. Both classical and quantum lattice decomposition cannot bypass the layer isolation. The multi-factor verification chain resists progressive brute-force exploration and resets iterative attack progress.

VWZ

VWZ is an original Vandermonde-structured rank-1 tensor lattice signature with security assumptions orthogonal to standard LWE. Its highly sparse structured public key achieves 8.5x compression with a current computational hardness beyond 22000, providing independent post-quantum redundancy even if conventional LWE assumptions are broken. The design avoids ring ideal leakage and offers minimal signature size with strong algebraic simplification resistance.

Scope & Limitations

Both modules are experimental deep-defense layers and do not replace standardized FIPS 203/205 primitives. They eliminate known algebraic shortcut attacks but do not defeat full-dimensional lattice reduction or runtime side-channel leakage. In independent researcher PQC innovation, the LookingGlass algebraic isolation framework and VWZ Vandermonde-structured rank-1 tensor lattice signature represent world-leading original work with complete theoretical construction, software implementation, FPGA verification, and timestamped traceable records.
