4. Post-Quantum Cryptography Disclaimer

  • Standardization Status: ML-KEM-768/1024 (FIPS 203) and ML-DSA-44/65/87 (FIPS 204) are NIST standards, but individual implementations (including this project's) may contain unknown vulnerabilities
  • Self-Implementation Risk: SM2-KDF, SM3, and related Chinese national cryptographic algorithms are self-implemented (referencing GB/T 32905-2016, GB/T 32918.4-2016) and have not been audited by a third-party cryptographic certification body
  • Side-Channel Attacks: SM2 implementation has passed TVLA side-channel verification (N=5,000/10,000, |t| < 4.5, 1st–4th order moments 20/20 PASS) with Montgomery Ladder triple protection against timing side-channels. This does not constitute resistance to physical attacks (power analysis, EM emanations), fault injection, or microarchitectural side-channels
  • Quantum Attack Evolution: Post-quantum cryptography is an active research field. Future advances in quantum computing may weaken current parameter security
  • Formal Verification Gap: Key constructions such as the Fujisaki-Okamoto (FO) transform have not undergone formal verification (EasyCrypt/Coq)

5. ZK Anonymous Login Research Statement

The zero-knowledge proof (ZK) anonymous login functionality is a cryptographic technology demonstration (Pedersen commitments + Schnorr ZK proofs). It does not fulfill legal real-name authentication requirements under the Cybersecurity Law of the People's Republic of China and is not a production-grade identity verification solution.
